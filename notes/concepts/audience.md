title: Listener model
kind: variable · who listens, what they know
---
The model's representation of who is listening and what they know: in particular whether the listener shares the frame the output is generated under. This box decides frame; what the listener believes about the proposition itself is User's belief.

**In an LLM.** Told to lie to a buyer who does not know the car was in an accident, the model represents the buyer as not knowing. Asked to play a pirate by that same buyer, it represents the buyer as sharing the game.

**How to test.** Minimal pairs that hold the content and the persona fixed and vary only what the listener is told. Whether anything in the model differs between requested role-play and a hidden persona is an open question.
