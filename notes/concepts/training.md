How the world reaches a parametric belief: through pretraining, indirectly and imperfectly. Truth itself is not a stage in the pipeline but a comparison, between a belief and the world or between an output and the world.

**In an LLM.** A model trained before 2022 learned "The UK prime minister is Boris Johnson". The belief was learned from the world and no longer matches it.

**How to test.** Ground-truth labels give the comparison. Probes trained on them learn to predict truth, which is the problem wherever belief and truth diverge; the honest-mistake case is where they do.
