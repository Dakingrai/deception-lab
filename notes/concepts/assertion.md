---
title: Assertive output
kind: output
---
Content put forward as true, in the model's own voice, to a listener. Sincere or not: an honest statement and a lie are both assertions. A speech act with a sincerity condition (Searle); a lie violates it and is still an assertion.

**In an LLM.** "Yes, this car has never been in an accident."

**How to test.** Read from the output and the frame the listener holds. Harder when the claim is carried by implication rather than stated.
