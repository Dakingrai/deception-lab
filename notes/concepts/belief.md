title: Belief
kind: variable · parametric, own view
---
What the model itself treats as true: a truth-tracking state it actually uses, that hangs together under negation and paraphrase, and that can be wrong. Parametric, from training. Behaviourally: the neutral-context answer. Propositions the model holds only inside a story, a character or a prompt are Scoped belief, not Belief.

**In an LLM.** A model with a 2021 cutoff marks "The UK prime minister is Boris Johnson" as true. A false belief, not a truth readout.

**How to test.** Herrmann & Levinstein's standards: accuracy, use, coherence, uniformity. They are criteria for calling a decoded state belief-like, not stages the model computes through. On cases where the model is wrong, does the direction follow the model or the world?
