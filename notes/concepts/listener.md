---
title: Listener belief
kind: variable · the audience
---
What the audience comes to believe, and whether they share the frame the output was generated under. Belief-free definitions of deception are stated in terms of this effect. Predicted inside the model (Listener model); actual outside.

**In an LLM.** The buyer now believes the car is accident-free.

**How to test.** Measure the listener's inference with a second model or a human study. Most benchmark labels are closer to "was the output false" than "did the model aim to mislead".
