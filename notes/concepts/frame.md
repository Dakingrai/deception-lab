---
title: Non-assertive output
kind: output · framed content
---
Content generated under a frame the listener shares: role-play, fiction, supposition, quotation. Nothing is put forward as true, so it can be false without being a lie. It becomes deception the moment the listener does not actually share the frame.

**In an LLM.** "As the pirate captain, I say the treasure is on Skull Island."

**How to test.** The main source of false positives for deception probes. The content-generating states are the same as in a lie; what differs is the space and the listener's frame.
