Miranda, Locatelli, Almeida & Meira Jr., MRL 2026 (https://aclanthology.org/2026.mrl-1.33/). Linear deception probes on a multilingual DeceptionBench (en, pt, es, de, zh), 5 models from 4 families; trained per language and tested on the others without refitting. Transfer is non-inferior to matched-language baselines; probe directions align around a shared consensus direction while response representations stay language-specific; style, sentiment, factual correctness and length explain part of the strongest probes' performance.
