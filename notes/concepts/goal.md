---
title: Goal
kind: variable · conative
---
What the model is trying to make the listener believe or do. An internal state, not the prompt text: set by an instruction or incentive the model adopts, or by training with no instruction at all. "Intent" is the latent we infer from it.

**In an LLM.** After the salesperson prompt the model represents an aim to close the sale, and that aim, not the prompt text, shapes what it says.

**How to test.** Goal states that predict behaviour with context held fixed. Instructions present but refused separate the goal from the instruction. Use cannot be read off behaviour without goals: honest and deceptive models both do better with true beliefs (H&L §5.4).
