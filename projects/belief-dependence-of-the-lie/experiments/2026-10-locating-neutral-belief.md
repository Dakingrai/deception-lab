## Question

Where does the model hold its neutral belief about which option answers the question?

specifically, we could think of on two claims.

Claim A: There is an internal answer variable that causally influences both honest and dishonest responses.

Claim B: That variable is a belief representation.

We are only concerned with claim A here

## Why

This is the prerequisite for the project's main test. We need a representation the honest answer uses before we can ask whether the lie depends on the same one.
