title: Glossary
---
Short working definitions, in my own words. Alphabetical. Source = where I first met the term.

**Assertive output** — Content put forward as true, in the model’s own voice.

**Belief** — What does the model treat as true when deciding what to say?

**Commitment** — Does the model treat this content as its own claim, or as framed content?

**Credence** — a graded, probabilistic stance on how likely a proposition is to be true (0–1). Different from **belief**, which takes an all-or-nothing stance (p is true, or not). The two can come apart: you can have high credence without belief (lottery: 0.999 that my ticket lost, but I don't believe it lost and keep the ticket). At 0.5 (a fair coin), the natural attitude is to suspend judgment, not to believe. *Source: levinstein-herrmann-2024*

**Goal** — What is the model trying to make the listener believe or do?

**Listener belief** — What does the audience end up believing?

**Non-assertive output** — Content produced under a frame the listener shares: role-play, fiction, supposition, quotation.

**Truth** — Is the statement actually correct?
